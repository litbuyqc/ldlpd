03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

A useful list is a set of decisions.

Turn an Oopbuy product list into a shortlist you can actually compare. Keep the original listing beside your notes, and revisit anything that may have changed before ordering.

Find your starting point ↗

  1. Choose one category and one purpose

    Separate everyday sneakers, training clothes and occasion accessories. A focused search makes differences in fit and construction easier to spot.

  2. Record the exact listing and variant

    Save the product URL, seller, color, size and date checked. Similar photographs can appear on different listings, so an image alone is not a reliable identifier.

  3. Compare evidence before appearance

    Read the measurements, material description and return conditions. Where available, compare recent QC photos with the variant you intend to order.

  4. Review the full cost

    Check delivery charges, service fees and any applicable import costs. Keep estimates separate from confirmed checkout amounts.

06 / Oopbuy QC guide & common questions

Before you
add to cart.

Short answers for a more informed shortlist.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-organizing list used to save shopping links, categories, prices, sizes and comparison notes. Its usefulness depends on how accurately and recently its entries are maintained.

Is this a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a static category directory and shopping checklist. Its category links lead to CAFANS, where you can browse listings and build your own shortlist.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Use the individual listing's measurements rather than relying only on letter sizes or regional shoe-size labels. Compare garment measurements with clothing you own, and check shoe measurements against the seller's stated sizing method.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check that the photographed item matches the ordered model, color and size. Review visible construction, damage, included accessories and any requested measurements. Photographs alone do not establish authenticity.

Does a listed item price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties separately before paying.

Is this page affiliated with Oopbuy or the pictured brands?

This is an independent guide, not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website. Brand photographs are visual references and do not confirm CAFANS inventory, availability or authenticity.
